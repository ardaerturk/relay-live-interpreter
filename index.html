Listening guide

Listen through your AirPods
Connect them in Control Center before you start. For a group, place the microphone near the people speaking. If your iPhone microphone appears below, try it with AirPods as the output.
Find inputs briefly opens the mic without sending audio.
Choose what you hear
Pick English or German. Speech is detected automatically. If someone already speaks your listening language, you may hear only their original voice in the room. Take turns for clearer voices.
Listening with the screen off
The iPhone app supports active audio in the background. Safari may pause its microphone when the phone locks. Return here to check the status.
Allow nearby voices to be sent
Let people know before starting. Live audio goes to Azure OpenAI. After Stop, tap Save to keep the translated words in your private History. Audio is never saved by Relay.
Add to your Home Screen
In Safari, open Share, then Add to Home Screen. Open the installed app before redeeming your device invite.
